# The realistic-lighting epic as a full lifecycle trace

One epic, every stage of the methodology, named artifacts only. Use this as the template for what "done right" looks like end to end. Committed and GitHub-hosted artifacts verified 2026-07-05. Two stage artifacts (the brainstorm packet and the end-to-end review notes) exist only on the owner's machine, untracked, and are absent from any fresh clone, worktree, or CI checkout; never cite them from a committed document. Their durable record is the committed and GitHub-hosted artifacts named in their rows.

## What the adversarial review changed (drift caught, decisions recorded)

| Review finding                                                                                                                         | Consequence, and where it is recorded                                                                                                              |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| The headline color-accuracy gate could pass on a direct-lit swatch while the real use case (a room lit by bounce) stayed untrustworthy | Sky and ambient-occlusion slices joined the spine; #451 states they sit there "because the headline gate tests an indirectly lit interior surface" |
| Sky image-based lighting had been deferred as a risky spike but was no longer risky                                                    | #436 re-scoped and shipped as PR #456 (ADR-0148)                                                                                                   |
| Ambient occlusion was the pragmatic interior-realism lever                                                                             | Filed as #442, shipped as PR #474 (ADR-0151), now closed                                                                                           |
| Tone-mapping research recommendation conflicted with landed ADR-0142                                                                   | Resolved as an owner decision in an explicit amending record, ADR-0147 (per-mode tone mapping)                                                     |
| Perceived-color readout was a locked brainstorm decision that reached no slice                                                         | Filed as #450, open, targeted alongside slice 3                                                                                                    |
| Sun-path and compass visualization dropped between brainstorm and spec                                                                 | Filed as #445, open                                                                                                                                |
| Night and moon scenes identified as cheap and mission-aligned                                                                          | Filed as #446, open                                                                                                                                |
| No luminance-calibration convention existed, so the materials slice would tune against an arbitrary anchor                             | Calibration ADR owed before slice 3 is tuned, tracked inside #449                                                                                  |
| Old promises with no owner (color-temperature presets, slider meaning per mode, WebGL2 parity posture)                                 | Parked verbatim on #451's retire-or-resurrect list, awaiting explicit fates                                                                        |

Every row above is a deferral or conflict that could have silently vanished. None did: each ended as a shipped slice, a numbered open issue, a recorded amending ADR, or a named parked entry. That is the whole method in one table.
